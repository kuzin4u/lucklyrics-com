How the model works

The engine is a five-stage funnel driving a per-period P&L. Every input recalculates the whole chain instantly, so you can find the lever combination that maximises net profit. It carries the same architecture as the Ozon version of this model, with the ranking formula, fee structure and penalty blocks rebuilt for Amazon.com.

Funnel math

  • Impressions × CTR = clicks; clicks × CR(cart) = add-to-cart; cart × CR(order) = orders.
  • Orders − cancellations = net orders; net orders − returns = delivered units.
  • Delivered units × repeat-rate = repeat units, added with no extra ad spend — the retention effect.
  • The bottleneck is the lowest-converting step; a small gain there yields the largest lift in total sales.
  • Capacity: if delivered demand exceeds stock or daily cap × period, sales and ads are clipped to the ceiling.

A10 ranking (2026)

Amazon has never officially renamed A9 to A10; "A10" is the seller community's shorthand for observed shifts since ~2020. The model reflects the current consensus: conversion rate is the strongest single factor, organic and external-traffic sales carry more ranking momentum than the same volume of Sponsored-attributed sales, and seller authority gates Buy Box eligibility. Exact weights are not published — the 65/35 organic/sponsored default is a modelling assumption, adjustable by slider for scenario analysis.

Adapting the model to any SKU

Nothing in the engine is tied to a particular product — the presets above (microfibre cloths, Bluetooth earbuds, face serum) just load different default values into the same fields. Switch preset to see how the economics shift: electronics carry a low 8% referral fee but a higher price, larger FBA tier and more returns; beauty runs a 15% fee above $10 but high margin and repeat rate; a household consumable is cheap, small-tier and volume-driven. To model your own SKU, set these inputs and everything recalculates:

InputWhat to enter
Sale price & landed costYour own figures in USD.
Referral fee %Your category's rate — 8% electronics, 15% most, 17% apparel >$20, up to 20% jewellery. Beauty & grocery are 8% under $10 and 15% above.
FBA size tierPick the tier matching your dimensions/weight; the fulfilment fee follows.
CTR, CR, returns, repeatFrom your Business Reports — the ranges differ a lot by niche.

This is the line between self-service and custom work. An ordinary physical FBA SKU is a matter of changing these numbers — a minute of typing. A product with a different structure — subscription-first economics, parent/child variations in one listing, FBM logistics instead of FBA, or seasonal demand peaks — needs the model extended, not just re-valued. That extension is where a bespoke build adds what a fixed template or an off-the-shelf tool cannot.
